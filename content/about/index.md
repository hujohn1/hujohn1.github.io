Hi! I'm John Hu, a third year Electrical Engineering student @ Duke with strong interests in EVs, power electronics, and analog circuit design. I currently act as the club president for Duke Hyperloop, where I lead, organize, and work with a team of students to make a concept Hyperloop system.

## Projects

### Scara Robot Arm

Designed and built a small-scale SCARA robot arm with a 5:1 reduction.

* Shoulder: NEMA 17 stepper with a GT2 timing belt
* Z-axis: lead screw and a second NEMA 17
* Arm: pancake NEMA 17
* Controls: Arduino Uno and a CNC shield
* Modeled in Fusion 360, printed in PLA, iterated on tolerances

### Miniature Cycloidal Drive

Designed and built a small-scale cycloidal drive using Fusion 360, PLA prints, and AliExpress ball bearings. The goal is a compact, high-ratio gearbox for robotics without relying on off-the-shelf planetary stages. Bench testing (torque and backlash) is still in progress.

### FPGA Dev with Tang Nano

First steps into FPGA development on the Sipeed Tang Nano (Gowin). Set up Gowin IDE, wrote Verilog for LED blink, flashed the bitstream, and verified on hardware. Next: custom logic blocks and timing constraints.
